# EXP-0001 — From session to trusted brief

**Status:** building

## Question

Can Hoomy turn a completed Codex session into a brief that is accurate, traceable, and quick to correct?

## Smallest product

The prototype reads one session selected by the user and produces a short brief containing:

- the intention;
- the outcome;
- unresolved questions;
- a possible next step.

Every important statement links to supporting evidence in the local session. Suggestions remain visibly different from facts. The user can accept, edit, or remove each statement.

## Pilot

Run the prototype locally on ten consecutive, usable personal sessions that the operator has the right to process. Do not use sessions containing employer, client, or third-party material, or credentials. The project supplies no public or synthetic session corpus.

For each brief:

1. check every important statement against its evidence;
2. rate the brief `accept`, `minor edit`, `major edit`, or `reject`;
3. record the review time and the main failure, if any.

This is a feasibility pilot, not evidence that the result generalizes to other people or agents.

## Continue or stop

Continue only if all three conditions hold:

- no fabricated quotation or unrelated evidence link;
- at least seven of ten briefs are accepted or need only a minor edit;
- median review time is at most 90 seconds.

If the pilot passes, publish the runnable prototype and design the next experiment: compare brief-first orientation with reopening the source session. If it fails, publish what failed and improve, redesign, or stop before expanding the product.

## Public result

Publish only the aggregate measures, observed failure categories, limitations, and decision. Never publish session text, project names, identifiers, paths, links, or per-session records.

**Result:** not run.
