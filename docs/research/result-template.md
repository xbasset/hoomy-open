# Research result template

Copy this structure into a proposed report. Replace every placeholder; use `not
measured` or `unknown` where appropriate. Publish only a reviewed, non-identifying
report, not the source evidence used privately during the experiment. See
[contribution guidance](../../CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Publication review

Confirm the report contains only material you may share. Remove source sessions,
source-bearing prompts, provider outputs/receipts, identifying metadata, credentials
and private paths. Check that small-sample aggregates do not reveal participants.
Record limitations rather than adding private evidence to make a claim look stronger.
The contributor reviews and authorizes the final diff and attachments; the agent
prepares the submission within that authority.
